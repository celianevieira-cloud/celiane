
Technological Modernization Project: Acquisition of Notebooks for the High School Ana Facó
1. Executive Summary

The High School Ana Facó faces limitations with old and static desktops. We propose the acquisition of 30 modern notebooks to replace these equipment, expanding mobility, flexibility and efficiency in teaching. It is expected to expand digital use in multiple school spaces, reduce operational costs and improve learning for students and teachers.
2. 2. Introduction and Justification

Currently, laboratories have outdated desktop computers with slow performance, high power consumption and restriction to a single environment. This limits the pedagogical use and access to technologies.

The introduction of notebooks will allow mobility between rooms, library, patio and external activities – fostering more dynamic, collaborative and student-centered methodologies. In addition, notebooks consume less energy, require less maintenance and occupy less space, optimizing the school infrastructure.
3. Objectives of purpose

    Promote mobility and flexibility in the use of computational resources.
    Increase the use of equipment by 80% in different school environments.
    Reduce electricity consumption by 30% compared to current desktops.
    Facilitate maintenance, upgrades and centralized management of equipment.
